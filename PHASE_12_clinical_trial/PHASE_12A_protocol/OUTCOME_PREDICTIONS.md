## Assumptions and Limitations

### Assumptions

1. **NOD2 mechanism:** Febuxostat stabilizes NOD2 LRR domain (based on MD simulation)
2. **Dose selection:** 80mg achieves therapeutic plasma concentrations
3. **Patient population:** Genotype-stratified selection enriches responders
4. **Placebo response:** 25% based on historical Phase II CD trials

### Limitations

1. **No prior clinical data:** Febuxostat has not been tested in CD
2. **MD simulation extrapolation:** In silico binding may not translate to clinical efficacy
3. **Heterogeneous disease:** CD pathophysiology involves multiple pathways beyond NOD2
4. **Small sample size:** Phase II powered for signal detection, not definitive proof
